Product photos for the device card, one folder per model, and the makers' logos.

eq35/ comes from Hoco's own Android app (hoco.music, com.hyst.hoco 2026-11): res/mipmap-xxhdpi-v4/icon_eq35.png
(the earbuds in the open case), icon_eq35_l.png (left) and icon_eq35_r.png (right). hoco-logo.png is the app's
res/mipmap-xxhdpi-v4/hoco_icon.png.

- eq35/*.png: the Windows app's card, 180 px tall (about 3x the 54 px they're shown at).
- eq35/hd/*.webp: the phone and tablet app, 420 px tall. case-plain.webp is the case with the hoco logo removed, for
  the screens shown before any earbuds are found.

The full-size originals are kept in artifacts/device-photos-original/ (git-ignored).
